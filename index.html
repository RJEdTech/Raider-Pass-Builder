Raider Pass Builder
Counseling passes · tomorrow's 1st-block rooms

Find any student's 1st-block room for tomorrow in seconds. Download tomorrow's schedule once, then type each name you see on the counselors' calendars.

🔒 The schedule file is read on this computer only. Nothing is uploaded or saved, and it's all gone when you close the page.

You're looking at example students (made-up names). Do Steps 1 and 2 to use real data.
1 Download tomorrow's schedule 2 Look up each student

STEP 1 Download tomorrow's schedule from myRJ

Do this once each afternoon. It saves a file to your Downloads folder that you'll use in Step 2.

  1. Open the report
    Click this red button. myRJ opens the Student Schedule For The Day report in a new tab. Sign in if myRJ asks.
    Open the report in myRJ ↗
  2. Type tomorrow's date
    Click in the Date box ①. Delete the date that's there and type tomorrow's date, like 10/26/2026. On a Friday, type Monday's date. Leave the other boxes as they are.
  3. Click View/Export, then Excel
    Click View/Export ②. A short list opens. Click Excel ③. The file saves to your Downloads folder in a few seconds. You can close the myRJ tab.

STEP 2 Add the file, then look up each student

Add the file once. Then type names one after another, as many as you need.

  1. Choose the file from Step 1
    Click the dashed box just below these pictures. A window opens on your Downloads folder. Click the newest file ending in .xls, then click Open. A line appears under the box with the date and how many students loaded ①. Check that the date is tomorrow.
  1. Type the name from the counselor's calendar
    Click in the Type a student's name box above and type part of the name, first or last. Spelling doesn't have to be perfect. The 1st-block room shows in big letters ②, with the building, the teacher and Red or White day. For the next student, click in the box again and type the next name. The old name is highlighted, so typing replaces it.
  2. Write it on the pass
    Put the teacher on Delivered to and the room on Rm. ③.

STEP 2 Send the counselors' calendars from Outlook

One button in Outlook sends every counselor's appointments for tomorrow to this page at once. You set the button up one time; after that it's three clicks.

One time only: add the Send to Pass Builder button

  1. Show your favorites bar
    Look just under the web address at the top of Edge. You should see a row of favorites like Mail, MyRJ and Canvas ①. If you don't, press Ctrl + Shift + B.
  2. Drag the button onto the bar
    Put your mouse on the dark Send to Pass Builder button below ②. Hold the left mouse button down, move the mouse up onto the favorites bar ③, and let go. The button now lives on your favorites bar.
    ➜ Send to Pass Builder
    Dragging didn't work? Add it by hand
    1. Click Copy the code below.
    2. Right-click an empty spot on the favorites bar and choose Add page (it may say Add favorite).
    3. In Name, type Send to Pass Builder.
    4. Click in URL, delete what's there, and press Ctrl + V.
    5. Click Save.

Each afternoon

  1. In Outlook, tick every counselor's calendar
    Open Calendar in Outlook. In the list on the left, under People's calendars, put a tick next to every counselor ①. You can see all of them at once; the button sorts out whose appointment is whose.
  2. Untick your own calendar
    Scroll up in the same list to My calendars and take the tick off Calendar ②, so your own meetings stay out.
  3. Go to tomorrow, in Day view
    At the top, click Day ③. Then click the › arrow ④ until the date shows tomorrow. On a Friday, go to Monday.
  4. Click Send to Pass Builder
    Click Send to Pass Builder on your favorites bar ⑤. A dark banner at the top of Outlook says how many appointments it sent ⑥, and this page opens in a new tab with them already in it.
The Pass Builder tab didn't open? Paste instead
  1. The button also copies the appointments. Come to this page.
  2. Click in the box below and press Ctrl + V.
  3. Click Add entries.

STEP 4 Check, then read the rooms

You don't have to print anything. The room list below is the answer; printing is there if you want it.

  1. Check anything yellow
    In Appointments found, green Ready rows are done. A yellow Confirm name ① means the calendar spelling was a little off: look at the student the builder picked and tick the box if it's right, or pick someone else from the list. Staff meetings and personal events are left out for you.
  2. Read the rooms
    Rooms for tomorrow lists every student with their 1st-block room, building, teacher, appointment time and counselor. Use Sort by ② to put it in the order that helps you: Walking order for delivering, Counselor, then time to match the calendars, or Last name to find someone fast.
  3. Print, only if you want to
    Print room list ③ prints this list on one page, in the order you picked. Print passes ④ prints every pass filled in, four to a page in walking order, with a route page first. In the print window, check the printer and click Print.
